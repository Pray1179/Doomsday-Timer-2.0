Avengers Roster History

Every era where characters are explicitly shown as Avengers or a formal Avengers roster. Allies and coalitions are labeled separately from the roster.

2012 Founding Team formal · Marvel's The Avengers

The original six. Nick Fury coordinates; he is not a roster member.

Members: Tony Stark / Iron Man; Steve Rogers / Captain America; Thor; Bruce Banner / Hulk; Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow; Clint Barton / Hawkeye

Age of Ultron Expansion formal · Avengers: Age of Ultron

A new compound and an expanded, explicitly Avengers roster.

Added: Wanda Maximoff; Vision; James Rhodes / War Machine; Sam Wilson / Falcon

Departed: Bruce Banner / Hulk (left Earth); Pietro Maximoff (deceased)

Civil War Split split · Captain America: Civil War

The Sokovia Accords fracture the team into two factions.

Team Iron Man: Tony Stark / Iron Man; James Rhodes / War Machine; Vision; Wanda Maximoff (house arrest); Peter Parker / Spider-Man; Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow (defects)

Team Cap: Steve Rogers / Captain America; Sam Wilson / Falcon; Clint Barton / Hawkeye (retired); Bucky Barnes / Winter Soldier; Scott Lang / Ant-Man; Wanda Maximoff

Infinity War Coalition coalition · Avengers: Infinity War

A battlefield coalition rather than one formal roster. The Avengers lose, and institutionally they remain divided.

Members: Tony Stark; Steve Rogers; Thor; Natasha Romanoff; James Rhodes / War Machine; Sam Wilson / Falcon; Wanda Maximoff; Vision; Peter Parker / Spider-Man; Stephen Strange & Wong (allies); The Guardians (allies); Black Panther & Wakandan forces (allies); Bucky Barnes (ally); Shuri & Okoye (allies)

Endgame Final Battle · Avengers: Endgame

Core six: Tony Stark; Steve Rogers; Thor; Bruce Banner; Natasha Romanoff; Clint Barton

Key allies: James Rhodes / War Machine; Scott Lang / Ant-Man; Rocket; Nebula; Carol Danvers / Captain Marvel

Restored: All Blipped heroes rejoin the final battle across the Multiverse.

Post-Endgame Status transition · The Falcon and the Winter Soldier → Captain America: Brave New World

Steve passes the shield to Sam Wilson, who becomes Captain America. The Avengers program is largely inactive until new leadership emerges.

New Avengers (after Thunderbolts*) formal · Thunderbolts*

Officially branded the NEW AVENGERS at the end of Thunderbolts*. Listed from released Thunderbolts* data only — do not assume every announced actor is a member.

Members: Yelena Belova; Bob Reynolds / Sentry; John Walker / U.S. Agent; Ava Starr / Ghost; Alexei Shostakov / Red Guardian
