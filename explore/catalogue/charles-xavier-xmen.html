Charles Xavier

Professor X

Portrayed by Patrick Stewart

X-Men Variant Hero Earth-10005 / Fox

Summary

The telepathic founder and leader of the X-Men who dreams of peaceful mutant-human coexistence. In the 2024 film Deadpool & Wolverine he abdicated his Earth-10005 Xavier's School responsibilities to the X-Men at large.
