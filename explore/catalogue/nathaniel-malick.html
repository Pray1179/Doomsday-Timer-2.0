Nathaniel Malick

Nathaniel Malick

Portrayed by Darren Barnet

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Deceased Villain Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

Gideon Malick's younger brother who uses the Chronicom time-travel technology to rewrite HYDRA's history and build his own version of S.H.I.E.L.D. He kidnaps and converts young Inhumans to serve him, but is killed when the team's final timeline correction erases his altered history.
