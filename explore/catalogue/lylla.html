Lylla

Lady Lylla

Portrayed by Linda Cardellini (voice)

MCU Dead Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A wise, kind otter and Rocket's closest companion from the High Evolutionary's experiments on Counter-Earth. She dreamed with Rocket of a free life among the stars, only to be gunned down before they could escape — the loss that still haunts Rocket.
