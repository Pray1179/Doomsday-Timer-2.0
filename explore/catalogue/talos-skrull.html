Talos

Talos the Skrull

Portrayed by Ben Mendelsohn

MCU Dead Support Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A Skrull general who once led his people against the Kree, Talos became an ally of Carol Danvers and later worked with Nick Fury, living disguised among humans on Earth. He was killed in Secret Invasion while trying to find his captured daughter.
