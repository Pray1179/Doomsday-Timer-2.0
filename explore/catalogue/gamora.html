Gamora

Gamora

Portrayed by Zoe Saldaña

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The last daughter of Thanos, trained as an assassin before defecting to the Guardians. Thanos sacrificed her on Vormir for the Soul Stone in 2018. A 2014-era variant displaced into the present during Endgame survives and finds a new home with the Ravagers.
