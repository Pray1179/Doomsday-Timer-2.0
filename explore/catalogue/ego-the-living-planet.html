Ego

Ego the Living Planet

Portrayed by Kurt Russell

MCU Deceased Villain Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

An ancient Celestial who is himself a living planet and the biological father of Peter Quill. He planned to terraform the universe in his image using Peter's power, and was destroyed by the Guardians in 2014.

Debut
