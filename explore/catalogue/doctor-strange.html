Stephen Strange

Doctor Strange

Portrayed by Benedict Cumberbatch

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A brilliant neurosurgeon who became Sorcerer Supreme's defender of reality after a car accident destroyed his hands. He wielded the Time Stone to bargain for half the universe, only for the Snap to still happen, and now guards the Multiverse.
