Odin

Odin Borson

Portrayed by Anthony Hopkins

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The Allfather of Asgard and father of Thor, Hela and Loki. He conquered the Nine Realms in his youth and later banished Thor to teach him humility, dying in Thor: Ragnarok with the truth about Hela left to his sons.
