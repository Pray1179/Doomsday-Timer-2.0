Drax

Drax the Destroyer

Portrayed by Dave Bautista

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A grieving warrior whose family was murdered by Ronan. He joins the Guardians seeking vengeance, and over time softens into a comic-relief father figure before joining the team's new era and eventually staying on Knowhere to raise the children.
