Daniel Sousa

Daniel Sousa

Portrayed by Enver Gjokaj

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A World War II veteran and original S.S.R. agent who is recruited by S.H.I.E.L.D. in 1946 and later pulled forward in time by the Chronicom war. He becomes a key member of Coulson's team and establishes the framework for the modern S.H.I.E.L.D.
