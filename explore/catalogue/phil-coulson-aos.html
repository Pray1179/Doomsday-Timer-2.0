Phil Coulson

Agent Coulson

Portrayed by Clark Gregg

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Unknown Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

S.H.I.E.L.D. agent resurrected after the Battle of New York using Tahiti protocol and Kree blood. He leads the S.H.I.E.L.D. team through HYDRA's fall, the Inhuman outbreak, the Framework, and the time-traveling Chronicom war, ultimately disappearing during the final mission.
