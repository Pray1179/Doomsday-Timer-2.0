Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A gifted S.H.I.E.L.D. agent and Inhuman with seismic powers who begins as a hacker named Skye. After undergoing Terrigenesis she becomes Quake, leads the Secret Warriors, and eventually takes over as field director of S.H.I.E.L.D.
