Groot

Groot

Portrayed by Vin Diesel (voice)

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A tree-like Flora Colossus who sacrifices himself to save his family in the first film, regrown from a twig as Baby Groot. The current Groot, now a full adult in Vol. 3, enters a new unlikely partnership with Rocket.
