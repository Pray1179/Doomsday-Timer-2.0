Peter Parker

Spider-Man

Portrayed by Tobey Maguire

Spider-Man (Raimi trilogy) Variant Hero Earth-96283 / Raimi

Summary

The shy, noble Peter Parker bitten by a genetically-enhanced spider in the Raimi trilogy. He fought the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus and Venom, and crossed into the MCU during the multiversal breach of No Way Home, where he reunited with his alternate-universe counterparts.
