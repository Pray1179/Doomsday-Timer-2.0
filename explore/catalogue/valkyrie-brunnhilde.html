Brunnhilde

Valkyrie

Portrayed by Tessa Thompson

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The last of the legendary Valkyries, a hard-drinking scavenger who survives Hela's purge. She helps Thor defeat Hela, fights the Black Order at the Battle of Earth, and is crowned the new King of New Asgard.
