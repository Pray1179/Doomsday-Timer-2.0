Peter Quill

Star-Lord

Portrayed by Chris Pratt

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

Half-human, half-Celestial abducted from Earth in 1988 and raised by the Ravager Yondu. Leader of the Guardians, he sacrificed his father Ego's power to save the galaxy. Blipped for five years after the Snap, then rejoined his found family before the Guardians' departure with a new team.
