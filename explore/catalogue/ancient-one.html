The Ancient One

The Ancient One

Portrayed by Tilda Swinton

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The centuries-old Sorcerer Supreme who protected Earth from dimensional threats. She denied Strange the Time Stone, then died defending Kamar-Taj, but appeared again during the Time Heist in Endgame.
