Melinda May

The Cavalry

Portrayed by Ming-Na Wen

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A legendary S.H.I.E.L.D. pilot and combat specialist haunted by the Bahrain incident. She is Coulson's most trusted operative, becomes Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. after the Chronicom war, and holds the team together through every crisis.
