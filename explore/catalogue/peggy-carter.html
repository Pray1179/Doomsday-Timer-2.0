Peggy Carter

Agent Carter

Portrayed by Hayley Atwell

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A founder of S.H.I.E.L.D. and the love of Steve Rogers's life. She co-founded the agency after World War II, was reunited with Steve in the 1970s timeline, and the 2019-era Steve chose to live out his life with her in the past.
