Venom

Venom (Symbiote)

Portrayed by Tom Hardy (voice)

Sony's Spider-Man Universe Active Anti-hero Earth-688 / SSU

Summary

The chaotic, hungry alien symbiote bonded to Eddie Brock. It speaks its own thoughts, feeds on chocolates and host brains, and left a fragment in the MCU when a variant was pulled through at the end of No Way Home.
