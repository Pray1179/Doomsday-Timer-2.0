T'Challa

Black Panther

Portrayed by Chadwick Boseman

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

King of Wakanda and the Black Panther who defended his nation and joined the fight against Thanos. He sacrificed himself to save Okoye and Wanda during the Outriders' assault, but was restored by the Blip reversal before his death in Wakanda Forever.
