Danny Rand

Iron Fist

Portrayed by Finn Jones

Netflix Marvel Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The last survivor of a plane crash, raised in the mystical city of K'un-Lun and granted the iron fist after defeating the dragon Shou-Lao. He returns to New York to reclaim Rand Enterprises, fights the Hand, and eventually renounces the iron fist to end their war.
