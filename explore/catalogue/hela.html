Hela

Goddess of Death

Portrayed by Cate Blanchett

MCU Deceased Villain Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

Odin's firstborn, the Goddess of Death, imprisoned for millennia for her conquest. Freed by Odin's death, she destroyed Asgard's armies and the Warriors Three before Surtur destroyed Asgard with her on it.
