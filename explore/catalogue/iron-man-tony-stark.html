Tony Stark

Iron Man

Portrayed by Robert Downey Jr.

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

Billionaire genius, philanthropist and founding Avenger who built the first functional Iron Man suit in a cave from scraps. Tony used the Infinity Stones in 2023 to undo the Snap and died of their power in the Battle of Earth.
