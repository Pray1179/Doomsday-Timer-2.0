Norman Osborn

Green Goblin

Portrayed by Willem Dafoe

Spider-Man (Raimi trilogy) Variant Villain Earth-96283 / Raimi

Summary

The brilliant, ruthless founder of Oscorp who split into a murderous Green Goblin persona after his experimental serum. He died at his own glider in 2002, but a variant crossed over in No Way Home and fatally wounded Aunt May.
