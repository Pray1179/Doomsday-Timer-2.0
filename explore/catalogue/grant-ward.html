Grant Ward

Grant Ward

Portrayed by Brett Dalton

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Deceased Villain Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A S.H.I.E.L.D. trainee who is secretly a HYDRA sleeper agent raised by John Garrett. His betrayal collapses the team, and he rises to become the new face of HYDRA after being stranded on Maveth. He is killed by Daisy but later inhabits a Framework copy and an LMD body before finally being destroyed.
