Yo-Yo

Portrayed by Natalia Cordova-Buckley

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A Colombian Inhuman who can accelerate to super-speed and bounce back to her starting point like a yo-yo. She joins the Secret Warriors, loses her forearms during the Chronicom war, and receives prosthetic replacements from Fitz.
