Yondu Udonta

Yondu

Portrayed by Michael Rooker

MCU Deceased Anti-hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The blue-skinned Ravager who abducted Peter Quill and raised him despite a fatherly bond he hid behind gruffness. He sacrifices himself to save Peter in the vacuum of space in Vol. 2 and dies a hero's death honored by the Ravager fleet.
