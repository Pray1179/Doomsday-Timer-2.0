Holden Radcliffe

Holden Radcliffe

Portrayed by John Hannah

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Deceased Villain Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A transhumanist bioethicist who builds the artificial intelligence A.I.D.A. and becomes obsessed with immortality through the Darkhold. He creates the Framework as a virtual paradise but loses control of his creation, eventually being killed by the Russian Ghost Rider.
