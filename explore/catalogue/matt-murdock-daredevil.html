Matt Murdock

Daredevil

Portrayed by Charlie Cox

Netflix Marvel Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A blind lawyer who fights crime at night with radar senses honed by a chemical accident that took his sight. He defends Hell's Kitchen as Daredevil, struggles with guilt and faith, and was pulled into the wider MCU with appearances in She-Hulk and Daredevil: Born Again.
