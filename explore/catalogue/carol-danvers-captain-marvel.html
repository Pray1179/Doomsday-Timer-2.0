Carol Danvers

Captain Marvel

Portrayed by Brie Larson

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A U.S. Air Force pilot who gained cosmic powers after an explosion exposed her to the Tesseract's energy. One of the MCU's most powerful heroes, she answers Nick Fury's pager in Endgame and later balances Earth defense with intergalactic responsibility.
