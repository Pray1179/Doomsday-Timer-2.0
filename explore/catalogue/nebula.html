Nebula

Nebula

Portrayed by Karen Gillan

MCU Active Anti-hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

Thanos's cybernetically rebuilt daughter who endured torture each time she lost to Gamora. She turns against her father, helps the Avengers in the Time Heist, and eventually finds a home with the Ravagers before rebuilding them in Vol. 3.
