Rocket

Portrayed by Bradley Cooper (voice)

MCU Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A genetically engineered raccoon-like weapons expert created by the High Evolutionary. He becomes the new leader of the Guardians after Adam Warlock nearly kills him, then takes a captain's chair as the team reboots in Vol. 3.
