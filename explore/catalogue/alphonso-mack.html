Alphonso Mackenzie

Mack

Portrayed by Henry Simmons

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Active Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

A former S.H.I.E.L.D. mechanic who becomes the team's heart, wielding his signature shotgun-axe. He leads the Resistance against the Framework's HYDRA regime, becomes Director of S.H.I.E.L.D. after the Chronicom war, and rebuilds the organisation in the epilogue.
