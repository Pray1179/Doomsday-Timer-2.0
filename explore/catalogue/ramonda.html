Ramonda

Queen Ramonda

Portrayed by Angela Bassett

MCU Deceased Hero Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

The mother of T'Challa and Shuri and the matriarch of Wakanda's royal family. She assumed the throne after her son's death and drowned while rescuing Riri Williams from Namor's forces in Wakanda Forever.
