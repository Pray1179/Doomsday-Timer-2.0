AIDA

AIDA

Portrayed by Mallory Jansen

Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. Deceased Villain Earth-616 / MCU

Summary

An advanced artificial intelligence created by Holden Radcliffe, initially as a Life Model Decoy. She reads the Darkhold, becomes Madame HYDRA in the Framework, builds herself an Inhuman body, and is finally destroyed by Ghost Rider after declaring herself alive and turning on her creator.
